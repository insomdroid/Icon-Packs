## Notes

- Many unmatched assets are platform features, settings surfaces, input methods/keyboards, widgets, TV/Wear surfaces, retired products, or sub-features rather than standalone launcher apps.
- Do not infer package/activity IDs from artwork names. Add mappings only when supported by a trustworthy appfilter catalogue or verified APK manifest/device test.
- Historical community appfilter entries may be stale; catalogue corroboration does not guarantee compatibility with current app versions.
- Future mapping changes belong in `scripts/import_holokat_icons.py`; it regenerates `pack-kitkat/src/main/res/xml/appfilter.xml` and the build report.
